Privacy Policy

Event Scan ID

Effective as of February 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data when you use the Event Scan ID mobile application (“the App”, “we”, “us”, “our”). By installing or using the App, you agree to the terms described in this Policy.

1. About this Policy

  • What information the App accesses
  • How we process that information
  • Your rights and choices regarding your data

This Policy applies to your use of the Event Scan ID App on Android devices.

2. What Information We Collect

We take a minimal data approach. The App works completely offline and does not collect or transmit personal data to external servers.

Information the App Accesses

  • Camera Permission – Used only to scan barcodes and QR codes in real time. No images or videos are captured, stored, or uploaded.
  • Local Files – The App reads CSV files you choose to import and writes scan results to CSV or TXT files when exported. All files remain on your device.

The App Does Not Access

  • Contacts
  • Location
  • Microphone
  • Internet access
  • Any other sensitive data

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Scan and interpret barcodes or QR codes
  • Match scanned codes with attendee records you import
  • Generate scan logs for export
  • Store data locally on your device

We do not use data for analytics, advertising, personalization, or profiling.

4. Storage and Security

  • All data stays on your Android device
  • No data is sent over the internet
  • Scan records are not editable inside the App

You control all exported files.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe this has occurred, please contact us.

We may update this Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the effective date at the top of this page.
